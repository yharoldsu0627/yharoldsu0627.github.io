01About

I am a BSc student in Computer Science and Software Engineering at Fuzhou University - Maynooth International Engineering College, with a GPA of 3.80/4.00.

My research focuses on 3D vision-language models, geometry-aware representation learning, 3D scene reconstruction, and remote sensing image-text retrieval. I work on methods that preserve geometric structure while aligning visual and language representations.
